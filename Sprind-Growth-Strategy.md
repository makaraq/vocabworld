## 4. Zero-Budget Growth Levers to Hit 100 Users

- **Seed circle:** Ask friends/family/former classmates to download + leave a rating. Aim for 15–20 here in week one — this also seeds your leaderboard/streak feature so it doesn't feel empty to new users.
- **Micro-influencer trades:** DM 5–10 nano creators (under 10K followers) in the langtok/travel space, offer free lifetime access in exchange for an honest reaction video. No cash needed — creators this size often say yes to product trades.
- **Community posting:** Share (don't spam) in relevant subreddits (r/languagelearning, r/Spanish, r/SideProject, r/AppHookup-style communities) and Facebook language-learning groups — lead with value ("built this because X frustrated me"), not a sales pitch.
- **Comment strategy:** Spend 20–30 min/day replying genuinely on top language-learning TikToks/Reels/tweets — a good reply with your face/app visible in your profile is discovery.
- **Link in bio:** Direct App Store link, always current, same across all 3 platforms.
- **Cross-post relentlessly:** Every TikTok becomes a Reel becomes an X clip same day. Triples reach for the same filming effort.
- **Ask every downloader for one thing:** a rating/review — social proof compounds new-user trust once you're past ~20 reviews.
