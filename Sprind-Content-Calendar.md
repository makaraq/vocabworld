# Sprind — 3-Week Content Calendar
Film each video once, post same-day to TikTok + Instagram Reels, then adapt caption for X. 15 core videos + daily X filler.

---

## WEEK 1 — Introduce the app, seed circle push

**Post 1 — Mon | Pillar: App in Action**
Hook (on-screen text, first 1s): *"I found the app that teaches you what to say in an actual emergency"*
Script: Open on you looking stressed/lost. Cut to screen recording — open Sprind, tap Emergency pack, tap "ayuda," audio plays "ayuda... help." Cut back to you saying it out loud, relieved.
Caption: "Downloaded this after almost not knowing how to ask for help on a trip 😅 it's called Sprind, teaches real phrases with native pronunciation. Link in bio 🔗 #langtok #spanish #travelspanish #languageapp"

**Post 2 — Tue | Pillar: Teach a Phrase**
Hook: *"3 Spanish phrases you need before you fly"*
Script: Quick cuts, you say each phrase, screen-record Sprind playing native audio right after each one for comparison.
Caption: "Save this before your next trip 📌 Learning these on Sprind rn, link in bio #learnspanish #travelspanish #langtok"

**Post 3 — Thu | Pillar: Relatable Humor**
Hook: *"Me trying to remember 'ayuda' the second I actually need it"*
Script: Comedic skit — panic, blank face, then cut to you calmly using Sprind's quiz mode days earlier, smug reveal you knew it all along.
Caption: "Practicing >>> panicking. #spanishlessons #languagelearning #langtok"

**Post 4 — Fri | Pillar: Build in Public (X-first, cross-post as talking clip)**
X Thread: "I built Sprind because every language app I tried taught me how to talk about pineapples before it taught me how to ask for help. Here's why that's backwards 🧵"
(3-5 tweet thread: problem → what Sprind does differently → screenshots → link)
TikTok/IG version: talk-to-camera 20s cut of thread's core point.

**Post 5 — Sun | Pillar: App in Action**
Hook: *"POV: you hit a 7-day streak and the app throws confetti at you"*
Script: Screen record streak/badge unlock moment, react genuinely.
Caption: "not me getting way too invested in a streak 😭 #languageapp #duolingo #langtok"

**X daily fillers (Mon–Sun, days without a video):** phrase-of-the-day tweet with audio-style spelling ("Today's Sprind phrase: 'una emergencia' — an emergency. Small word, could save your trip."), or a screenshot of app progress.

## Notes
- Swap "Spanish" language pack details in scripts if Sprind supports other languages — keep whichever is most built-out front and center.
- Every caption ends with a link-in-bio CTA except where the hook itself is the CTA (milestone post).
- If a video underperforms (<500 views in 24h), don't remake it — just move to the next; if a format overperforms, repeat that format the following week before inventing a new one.
